F1                      a  these keys (video pauses)          same
space p                 a  pause, play; stopped: from 0 s     -
h l Left Right          a  seek -5 s, +5 s                    previous, next image; zoomed: pan
j k Down Up             a  seek -60 s, +60 s                  turn left, right; zoomed: pan
Shift+Left Shift+Right  a  seek -1 s, +1 s                    zoomed: pan, a sixteenth
Shift+Down Shift+Up     a  seek -5 s, +5 s                    zoomed: pan, a sixteenth
[ ] { }                 a  speed /1.1, x1.1; /2, x2           -
Backspace \             a  speed 100 %                        Backspace: zoom to the fit
9 0 / *                 a  volume -2, +2                      -
m                       a  mute                               -

H J K                      -                                  zoomed: pan, a sixteenth

r                          render page; F1 there: its keys    same

v                          -                                  image (kitty) or ASCII
y                          -                                  copy it (PNG); after D: delete
d                          -                                  trash it, show the next
u                          -                                  undo the last trash
D                          -                                  delete for good, after y

With the folder's last image gone only u, q, f and F1 act; after D, any key but y keeps the image.
